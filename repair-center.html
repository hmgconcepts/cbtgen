4 — “I uploaded the new build but the site still looks old”

That is the browser service-worker cache, not the deployment. After a redeploy, the previous version can serve until the new service worker installs (usually the second navigation). Since Phase 12L the platform shows a 🚀 A new version is available — Refresh now pill when this happens.

To see the fresh version immediately: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) — or DevTools (F12) → Application → Service Workers → Update / Unregister. Verify you are on the new build: the footer of any governance page, or sw.js in DevTools, should read hmg-cbt-shell-v12-phase12l-v1.
